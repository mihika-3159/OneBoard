# Draft demo privacy and acceptable-use notice

Not published or enforced by deployment. Intended for review before any real-data pilot.

OneBoard's hackathon demonstration uses synthetic business records and is not intended for real customer, financial, identity or payment information. Participants should not upload confidential documents, personal data, access tokens or banking credentials. AI-generated recommendations are advisory and can be incomplete or incorrect. Financial comparisons describe supplied or synthetic records, not certified accounting or bank verification. External sending, live charges and transfers are not part of this demonstration.

The application supports workspace access controls, retention and deletion, archive export and limited configured external integrations. The actual retention schedule, hosting locations, external processing providers, contact information and applicable legal terms must be determined before publishing a privacy policy or enabling real-data use. No live provider or third-party processing is authorized by this draft.
