# Dubai AI Agents Championship: OneBoard

**Category:** Technology & AI. **Tagline:** Run a company of one with the capabilities of a team.

Solo founders must coordinate business decisions, cash flow, client commitments and operations without a full executive team. OneBoard brings these tasks into a single workspace, with nine specialized advisory roles, evidence-linked deliberation, deterministic financial calculations and controlled workflow execution.

## Demonstrable prototype

- Review tenant-owned business records and explicit financial provenance.
- Convene a council and compare adviser positions, then record a founder decision and later outcome.
- Design and version a workflow, inspect approvals and recover eligible interrupted runs.
- Configure a custom agent with explicit read-only permissions and checkpointed steps.
- Import and search business documents, inspect citations, reconcile financial evidence and manage provider authorization where configured.

The prototype supports local/server-side Ollama. If genuine inference is unavailable, fallback output is clearly identified. Demo records and sandbox integrations are synthetic. Financial comparisons are not independent bank verification; external delivery, hosting availability, privacy compliance and external audits require their own validation. This is not a production-certified SaaS or an autonomous replacement for professional advice.

Evaluation should use disposable test data and the actual demonstrated paths. See TEST_REPORT.md and the release engineering documentation for scope, evidence and limitations.
